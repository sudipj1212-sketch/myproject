Our Services

Gangasagar Eco Tourism, a unit of Sagar Suraksha Welfare Foundation, is a local tourism initiative dedicated to providing safe, comfortable and responsible travel experiences in Gangasagar and the Sundarban region. We offer pilgrimage tours, Mela packages, private ferry services, local transportation, accommodation, guided tours, Sundarban water safaris and customised travel experiences, especially designed to support senior citizens, families, women travellers and pilgrims.
